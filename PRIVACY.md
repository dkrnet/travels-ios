# Travels Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Travels is a privacy-first location-history application. It is designed to keep personal location history under the user's control, and does not require an account.

## Information Travels handles

Travels stores location coordinates, timestamps, movement information, notes, imported photo metadata and attachments, place information, and app settings. This information is stored locally in the app's container on the user's device.

Travels does not include advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, remote logging, or a developer-operated service that receives location history. Travels does not sell personal information.

## Apple platform services and maps

Map View is disabled by default. While it remains disabled, Travels does not initialize the map view or request map tiles. If the user enables Map View, Travels downloads map tiles from Apple using standard Apple map APIs, and Apple receives the network requests needed to provide the map areas viewed. Optional address lookup sends coordinates to Apple only when enabled. Apple's handling of map requests and address lookups is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

The app also uses standard Apple platform services for location permissions, background location delivery, photo selection, device-owner authentication, and Shortcuts.

## Crash reports and beta testing

Users of the released App Store version are not required to provide crash logs and should decline if prompted by the system to share diagnostics. Beta testers who install Travels via TestFlight agree through Apple's TestFlight program to submit crash reports and feedback to the developer to help diagnose issues.

## User-controlled sharing

Data leaves Travels when the user explicitly exports a location file, database backup, or other app-generated file. The user chooses the destination of, and is responsible for protecting, exported data.

## Retention and deletion

Travels retains its local data until the user deletes events, removes the database, or deletes the app and its data from the device. iCloud or computer device backups may retain app data according to the user's Apple backup settings.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with the Travels source repository and reflected in the app's privacy disclosures where required.

## Questions and security reports

Privacy questions and security reports may be submitted through the contact methods documented in the Travels source repository. Do not include precise location history, addresses, photos, GPX files, or database backups in a public issue.
