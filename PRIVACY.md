# Travels Privacy Policy

Travels is a privacy-first location-history application. It is designed to keep personal location history under the user's control and does not require an account.

## Information Travels handles

Travels may store location coordinates, timestamps, movement information, notes, imported photo metadata and attachments, place information, and app settings. This information is stored locally in the app's container on the user's device.

Travels does not include advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, remote logging, or a developer-operated service that receives location history. Travels does not sell personal information.

## Optional Apple platform services

Address lookup is optional and sends coordinates to Apple only when enabled. Apple's handling of those requests is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

The app also uses standard Apple platform services for location permissions, background location delivery, photo selection, device-owner authentication, Shortcuts, maps, and TestFlight feedback where applicable.

## User-controlled sharing

Data leaves Travels when the user explicitly exports or shares a location file, database backup, maintenance log, photo attachment, or other app-generated file. The user chooses the destination and is responsible for protecting exported copies.

## Retention and deletion

Travels retains its local data until the user deletes events, replaces or removes the database, or deletes the app and its data from the device. iCloud or computer device backups may retain app data according to the user's Apple backup settings.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with the Travels source repository and reflected in the app's privacy disclosures where required.

## Questions and security reports

Privacy questions and security reports may be submitted through the contact methods documented in the Travels source repository. Do not include precise location history, addresses, photos, GPX files, or database backups in a public issue.
